Fittel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fittel collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects information when you use the Fittel mobile app, website, and related services (the "Service"). It also explains the choices and rights available to you.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Fittel ("we", "us", or "our") is currently operated by Ting Teck Chuan as an individual in Malaysia and is the controller of personal data described in this policy. Contact us at fittelbusiness@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • We collect account, profile, meal, workout, social, game-progress, and technical data needed to operate Fittel.
  • If you connect your device health service, permitted daily activity aggregates are read for insights and step-based quests. We do not write health data.
  • Meal photos and meal descriptions are processed through Fittel Intelligence to identify food and estimate nutrition. They are not used to train AI models by default.
  • Store and subscription services process purchase, receipt, entitlement, device, and subscription-status information for Fittel Premium. We do not receive your complete payment-card number.
  • Promotional or manually granted Premium access is stored against your Fittel account and is separate from an app-store purchase.
  • With foreground location permission, Fittel can add your current area to a meal. You can edit or omit it, and Fittel does not track location in the background.
  • If you use Share & Earn Premium, we process the public post link or screenshot and Profile email you submit so we can verify and grant the reward.
  • Friend connections do not expose meal or nutrition records. Trainer-community posts are visible to members of that trainer's community.
  • We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.
  • You can request account deletion inside the app or through our public account-deletion page.

4. How we obtain information

We receive information:

  • directly from you when you create a profile, log a meal, post content, submit feedback, or use another feature;
  • from your trainer or client when they create workouts, schedules, notes, offline-client records, or other coaching content involving you;
  • from identity, notification, app-store, and subscription services when you authenticate, receive a notification, make or restore a purchase, or when subscription status changes;
  • from your device health service only after you grant permission;
  • from your device location service only while you log or edit a meal and only after you grant permission; and
  • automatically from the app and backend when you interact with Fittel.

5. How we use information

We use personal data to:

  • provide, personalise, synchronise, and maintain Fittel;
  • authenticate accounts and protect users and the Service;
  • process meal analysis and calculate nutrition and fitness insights;
  • operate trainer-client, friend, community, quest, reward, and subscription features;
  • send service, coaching, friend, scheduling, security, and other notifications you permit;
  • process purchases, verify entitlements, and provide support;
  • investigate bugs, abuse, fraud, safety issues, and legal claims; and
  • comply with law and enforce our Terms of Use.

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, our legal bases may include performing our contract with you, your consent (including health and location permissions), our legitimate interests in operating and securing Fittel, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent, but this does not affect processing already completed and may prevent the related feature from working.

6. Fittel Intelligence and nutrition processing

Fittel Intelligence processes the meal images you select and the meal descriptions you provide to identify likely food items and estimate nutrition. It does not receive your name, email address, device health record, friend list, or trainer-client messages as part of the meal-analysis request.

Fittel uses a contracted automated-analysis service for image and text analysis. Its API inputs and outputs are not used to train public models by default and may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, unless a different approved retention control or legal requirement applies.

After you review or edit the detected foods, Fittel may send food names, portion information, and nutrition search terms—without your Fittel account identity or meal photo—to a food-reference service to improve nutrient matching. Fittel stores the final meal log and media you choose to save. Abandoned meal-analysis drafts and their uploaded photos expire after approximately two hours and are processed by an automated cleanup.

7. Device health data

Device health access is optional and read-only. With permission, Fittel reads supported daily steps, walking or running distance, active calories, total calories, and exercise minutes. Relevant daily aggregates may be transmitted to Fittel's backend to generate the insight you request and update step-based quests; daily step totals and quest completion may be retained. We do not request write access, read unrelated health categories, use health data for advertising, or show Health data to your trainer or friends.

Revoke access at any time in your device health or permission settings. Revocation stops future reads but does not automatically delete daily aggregates already saved by Fittel; account deletion removes account-linked quest and game records from active Fittel systems.

8. Subscriptions

Your device's app store processes your payment. Fittel uses a subscription service to display offerings, validate transactions, determine trial eligibility, restore purchases, and keep Premium access aligned with store status. Fittel sends a unique Fittel client ID to that service. It may process device and operating-system information, IP-derived location, last-seen information, receipt data, and purchase and subscription history. We do not send it your meal photos or device health data.

Fittel stores a limited subscription projection—such as product, store, environment, status, and expiration—to enforce Premium access. Store and subscription services retain transaction records under their own terms and legal obligations. Deleting Fittel does not cancel a subscription or erase transaction records those services must retain.

Fittel may also grant Premium access manually, including rewards earned through Share & Earn Premium. These grants are stored against your Fittel account with their source, status, and expiration. They are not app-store transactions and are not sent to the store as purchases. Consequently, Restore Purchases restores eligible store purchases only; it does not restore a manual grant to a different Fittel account.

If you buy a verified app-store subscription while a manual grant is active, Fittel marks the manual grant as replaced and uses the paid subscription as the source of Premium access. We retain the resulting entitlement records as needed to operate access, provide support, prevent duplicate rewards, and maintain transaction integrity.

To request a Share & Earn reward, you choose to join Fittel's messaging group and submit the public post link or screenshot together with the email shown in your Fittel Profile. The messaging service processes information associated with your account under its own policies. The information you post in the group may also be visible to other group participants, so do not submit anything you are not comfortable sharing there. Fittel uses the submission only to communicate with you, verify the displayed reward conditions, grant Premium, and prevent abuse.

9. Who can see your information

  • Linked trainer: can receive your name, email address, optional phone number, profile photo, goals, date of birth, sex, height, weight, activity level, calorie and macro targets, meal logs and nutrition estimates, assigned workouts and completion, schedules, off-days, and content you post in that trainer's community. A trainer does not receive your device health totals, friend list, private friend goals, or personal daily-insight text through the trainer interface.
  • Linked clients: a trainer can see records needed to coach that client. Clients do not automatically see another client's private meal, workout, or personal-stat data.
  • Accepted friends: can see limited profile information and Goal Buddy content you choose to share. Friendship alone does not expose meal logs, nutrition information, or device health data.
  • Trainer community: the trainer and clients linked to that trainer can see community-post text or media, author name, and profile photo.
  • External share recipients: if you export a meal card, workout card, invite, or other content through your device's share sheet, the destination app and recipient receive the content you selected. Their handling is outside Fittel's control.

10. Service providers and disclosures

We disclose data to service providers only as needed to operate Fittel:

  • Identity and notification services — account authentication, app installation records, and push-notification delivery.
  • Device platform services — optional sign-in, app-store purchases, subscription management, device health, and foreground location under your device settings.
  • Subscription processing — offerings, validation, receipts, trial eligibility, entitlement status, and purchase restoration.
  • Communication services — optional Share & Earn group participation and submission of a public post link or screenshot and Profile email.
  • Automated analysis and reference services — meal-photo and meal-description analysis, food and nutrition matching, and exercise catalogue media.
  • Database, storage, and hosting services — application records, uploaded media through access-controlled URLs, backend hosting, and operational logs.
  • Email delivery services — delivery of one-time sign-in codes using the destination email address and message.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganisation, or transfer of the Service. Where required, we will provide notice and require the recipient to protect personal data consistently with applicable law.

We do not sell personal data, share it with data brokers, or disclose it for targeted advertising across unrelated apps or websites.

11. Device permissions

  • Camera: used when you choose to take a meal or profile photo.
  • Photo library: used to access only the image or video you select for a meal, profile, custom exercise, or community post.
  • Notifications: used for reminders and service, coaching, scheduling, friend, reward, or account-related alerts.
  • Location: used in the foreground to add an optional area or selected place to a meal. Fittel does not continuously track location.
  • Device health: used as described above.

You can deny or revoke permissions in device settings. Core account features remain available, but the feature requiring that permission may not work. Fittel does not request microphone access.

12. Retention

  • Account and feature data: generally retained while your account exists or as needed to provide the Service.
  • Meal capture drafts: drafts can be stored in app-private device storage until you save or delete them. Unsynced local drafts are limited to approximately 100 MB. Temporary server-side analysis drafts and uploaded images expire after approximately two hours and are handled by automated cleanup.
  • Automated-analysis data: API inputs and outputs may be retained by the contracted service for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring unless a different approved control or legal requirement applies.
  • Email sign-in: one-time codes expire after 10 minutes. We store a one-way hash rather than the plaintext code; verified codes are removed, while security and rate-limit records may remain for a limited period.
  • Operational logs: retained for a limited period appropriate for security, debugging, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.
  • Premium and reward records: paid-subscription projections, manual entitlement status, expiration, and Share & Earn verification records are retained while needed to provide access, support users, comply with accounting or legal obligations, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims.
  • After account deletion: account-linked database records and access are removed from active Fittel systems. Residual copies can remain temporarily in backups, logs, isolated object storage, or service-provider systems until backup rotation, lifecycle cleanup, or legally required retention completes. Authentication data may take up to 180 days to leave live and backup systems. Store and subscription services may retain purchase records independently.

We may retain information longer where reasonably necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, or protect users. Where possible, retained information is restricted or de-identified.

13. Account deletion

Open Profile → Delete Account in Fittel to request deletion. Client deletion removes client-owned application records, meal history, friendships, community posts, game progress, Fittel subscription projections, manual Premium grants, active sessions, and the authentication identity from active Fittel systems. Trainer deletion removes trainer-owned records, communities, schedules, invites, and library data; prescribed workouts and diet history may remain with the client but become unattributed so the client keeps their own history.

Account deletion does not cancel an app-store subscription. Manage or cancel the subscription through your device's subscription settings before deleting your account if you do not want future renewal.

If you cannot access the app, use our public account-deletion page to submit a request.

14. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access and receive a copy of personal data;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete personal data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent;
  • receive portable data where applicable; and
  • complain to your local privacy or data-protection authority.

Many profile details can be viewed or changed in Fittel. Use the in-app deletion control or email fittelbusiness@gmail.com for other requests. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where law permits or requires it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

15. Security

Fittel uses HTTPS, authenticated API requests, access controls, rate-limiting, short-lived media URLs, and provider security controls. Email one-time codes are stored as one-way hashes, and Fittel does not store full payment-card details. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Contact us if you believe your account or data has been compromised.

16. International transfers

Fittel and its providers may process data in Malaysia, the United States, the Asia-Pacific region, and other countries where providers operate. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on contractual protections or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

17. Children

Fittel is not directed to children under 13, and a child under 13 must not create or use their own Fittel account. A trainer must not create an offline client record for a person under 13 unless the trainer has lawful authority, has obtained all required parental or guardian permissions, and applicable law permits the processing. If you believe we hold a child's data without valid authority, contact us and we will investigate and delete it where appropriate. Users below the age of legal majority must have parental or guardian permission where local law requires it.
